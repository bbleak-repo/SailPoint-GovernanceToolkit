Access Review Quick Reference

Most reviews take 5-10 minutes  |  You cannot break anything before Sign Off
Step by Step
  1. 1
    Click the link in your email or navigate to [YOUR-ORG.identitynow.com]
  2. 2
    Go to Certifications and open the Active tab -- find your campaign by name
  3. 3
    Click a team member's name to see all of their access items listed
  4. 4
    For each access item, choose: Approve (checkmark), Revoke (X), or Reassign (arrow)
  5. 5
    Add a brief comment for any PRIVILEGED-flagged items or items you are revoking
  6. 6
    Click Sign Off then Finish once all items in the campaign are decided
The Three Buttons
[+]
Approve
Yes, keep this access
Use when access matches the person's current role and they actively use it
[X]
Revoke
No, remove this access
Use when access is no longer needed, is too broad, or was from a previous role
[>]
Reassign
Send to someone else
Use when you do not recognize the system or the correct reviewer is another manager
What the Flags Mean
PRIVILEGED Elevated risk -- admin or sensitive access. Review carefully and add a comment with your decision.
NEW Recently added to the person's profile. This is the primary reason this item appears in your review.
AUTO-ASSIGNED System-managed role. You can only Acknowledge -- you cannot revoke. Contact the IAM team if concerned.

Common Scenarios
  • New hire has admin or elevated access Revoke admin; approve basic access
  • Access left over from a previous role Revoke the old access
  • You don't recognize the system or app Reassign to system owner
  • Access clearly fits the current role Approve + brief comment
