Callout Legend -- What to Look For
1
Subject line
Search your inbox for "Action Required: Access Review" -- this is how all SailPoint campaign notifications are labeled. Check your Junk folder if you do not see it.
2
Campaign name
The exact name you will see after clicking through to SailPoint. "Daily AD Delta Cert" identifies this as an AD-change-driven review, not an annual campaign.
3
Item count
The number of identities and access items you need to certify. Delta campaigns are small by design -- typically 1-10 items per reviewer, taking 5-10 minutes.
4
Deadline
Complete your review before this date. Overdue campaigns may be auto-completed by the IAM team or escalated. Uncertified access may be auto-revoked per policy.
5
"Review Now" button
Click to go directly to your open campaigns in SailPoint. You will be prompted to log in with your corporate SSO credentials if not already signed in.
6
Direct URL
Use this link if the "Review Now" button does not work in your email client. Bookmark [org].identitynow.com/ui/certifications for quick access.
2
Ready-to-Send Communication Templates
Three templates for the IAM team. Copy the subject and body text, customize the highlighted placeholders, and send via your standard email client. Placeholders appear as [LIKE THIS].
Template A -- Pre-Launch Announcement
Send 1 week before go-live
Audience: All managers who will receive certification campaigns
Purpose: Set expectations before the first campaign arrives -- reduces help desk tickets
Send at least 5 business days before the first daily delta cert goes live. Include a link to the Manager Attestation Guide if available.
Subject line
Starting [DATE, e.g. June 12]: Daily Access Reviews for Privileged Permissions
Email body
Hello, Starting [DATE], you will begin receiving periodic email notifications from SailPoint (noreply@sailpoint.com) asking you to review access for your direct reports. WHAT IS CHANGING? Our Identity Security team has enabled Daily AD Delta Certifications. This means that whenever a member of your team is granted elevated or privileged access in Active Directory, you will receive a short certification request -- usually 1 to 5 items -- to confirm that access is appropriate. This replaces the annual bulk review for these specific access types. Each review should take 5 to 10 minutes. WHAT YOU NEED TO DO When you receive an email with the subject "Action Required: Access Review," click the "Review Now" button and log in with your corporate credentials. For each identity listed, choose one of two options: - Certify: The access is appropriate. No action is taken. - Revoke: The access should be removed. IT will process the removal automatically. You will have 48 hours to complete each review. If you do not respond, the IAM team will follow up. [YOUR NAME] [TITLE] Identity and Access Management [COMPANY NAME]
Template B -- First Campaign Notification
Send with the first live campaign
Audience: Managers receiving their first real certification campaign
Purpose: Accompany the SailPoint system notification with a human touchpoint and quick-start guidance
Send on the same day the campaign activates. Send from a real IAM team address (not a no-reply) so managers can reply with questions.

Template C -- Missed Deadline Reminder
Send when campaign is overdue
Audience: Managers who have not completed a campaign past its deadline
Purpose: Prompt completion while communicating consequences of continued inaction
Send within 24 hours of the deadline passing. Escalate to the manager's director if no response is received within another 24 hours. Consult your policy before revoking access automatically.
