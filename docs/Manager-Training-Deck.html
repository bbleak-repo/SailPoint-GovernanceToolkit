Context

Why Are We Here?

When someone on your team gets new elevated access, you'll review it within 24 hours
Old Way
Quarterly reviews.
100+ items per batch.
Rubber-stamping risk -- too much to evaluate carefully.
New Way
Daily reviews.
1-5 items per session.
Focused, informed decisions on each access grant.
Time Investment
5-10 minutes per review session.
Only triggered when someone on your team receives new access.

Your Decisions

The Three Actions

✓
Approve
Yes, keep this access
Use when: the person uses this system for their current job and the access level is appropriate.
✗
Revoke
No, remove this
Use when: no longer needed, too broad, person changed roles, or a lower-privilege option exists.
→
Reassign
Send to someone else
Use when: you do not recognise the system, or the person works in a different business area.
None of these changes take effect until you click Sign Off -- you can revise any decision before then.

Worked Examples

Common Scenarios

New hire with admin-level access
Revoke Admin / Approve Standard
New hires should start with least-privilege. Revoke elevated items; approve what fits their role. IT can re-provision if genuinely needed.
Access left over from a previous role
Revoke
If the person moved teams or changed responsibilities, old system access no longer applies. Revoke the leftovers and note the reason.
"I only use this in emergencies"
Revoke
Standing privilege for rare events is unnecessary risk. Revoke it; contact IAM to set up on-demand (break-glass) access for genuine emergency scenarios.
Access you do not recognise
Reassign
Reassign to the system owner listed in the campaign. Never approve something you do not recognise -- that is exactly what Reassign is for.
Completing Your Review

Signing Off

Review
Decide
Summary
Sign Off
Done
Before Sign Off
Click any item and choose Revisit Decision to change your mind. Nothing has been actioned yet. All decisions remain editable.
After Sign Off
Decisions are final and recorded in the audit log. Revokes are actioned automatically. Contact the IAM team if a correction is needed.
Deadline
Complete your review within 24-72 hours of receiving the notification. Overdue items escalate to the IAM team automatically.
Before You Worry

You Cannot Break Anything

Nothing changes until you click Sign Off
  • Before Sign Off: click Revisit Decision on any row to change your mind as many times as you like.
  • After Sign Off: contact the IAM team -- mistakes are fixable. Revokes can be reversed by re-provisioning access.
  • Not sure? Use Reassign rather than guessing. That is the correct answer when you lack context.
